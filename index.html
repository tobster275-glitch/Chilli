Spicy things, written in code.

The story behind the name, the symbols and Material Keys.

Why chilli?

ch://i is pronounced “chilli”. I love anything spicy, so the company is named after something hot.

Why ://?

I’m really into coding, so the name borrows :// from web addresses. It’s inspired by the Mozilla logo, moz://a. Mozilla and Firefox are among my favourite companies.

Where Material Keys comes from

Keyboards like Gboard and SwiftKey have boring designs and collect your data aggressively. What if you could have Gboard + no data collection, tons of customization and an actually Material design like Google promised?
